## Повний опис (4000 символів, ліміт)

### EN

```
Read real English books without drowning in a dictionary.

Sapphire is a bilingual parallel reader: the English text and the translation sit
side by side, sentence by sentence. You read at your own pace and understand every
line — no jumping between apps, no losing your place.

WHAT YOU GET
• Parallel reading — English original + translation, one sentence per line
• Tap any sentence to translate it instantly (44 target languages)
• Built-in dictionary — every word you look up is saved automatically
• Flashcards with spaced repetition (FSRS) built from the words you actually met
• Import your own books: EPUB, FB2, MOBI, DOCX, ODT, RTF, HTML, TXT, ZIP
• Speed reading mode, text-to-speech, adjustable font and themes (Day / Night / Blackout)

WHY IT IS DIFFERENT
The dictionary and the flashcards are filled from YOUR book, not from a generic list.
You only revise words you genuinely encountered — and you delete the ones you already
know instead of confirming them forever.

Everything is stored on your device. No account, no tracking, no server.
```

## Що НЕ обіцяти

- Не обіцяти «вивчення англійської за 30 днів» — це не курс, а інструмент читання.
- Не обіцяти офлайн-переклад — переклад речень іде через мережу (Google GTX / Lingva).
- Не обіцяти безкоштовність назавжди — є банер AdMob + rewarded (поддержка автора).
