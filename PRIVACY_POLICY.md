# Privacy Policy — BookReader

**Last updated:** 2026-08-11

BookReader ("the App") is an offline-first e-book reader. This policy explains what
data the App handles and with whom it is shared.

## 1. Data we collect
- **None by default.** Books you add, reading progress, bookmarks, dictionaries, and
  app settings are stored **locally on your device** (via AsyncStorage). They are never
  uploaded to our servers, because the App has no backend server.
- We do **not** collect your name, email, location, contacts, or files.

## 2. Third-party services
- **Google AdMob** (advertising). The App may show ads. AdMob (Google) may collect:
  - Advertising ID (device advertising identifier),
  - Approximate device information (device type, OS version),
  - Interaction with ads.
  Google uses this to show personalized or non-personalized ads. You can reset/limit the
  Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
- AdMob may set and read advertising cookies/identifiers as described in
  Google's Privacy Policy and the EU User Consent Policy.

## 3. Children
The App does not knowingly target or collect data from children under 13.

## 4. Your choices
- You can opt out of personalized ads via your device ad settings.
- Removing the App deletes all local data.

## 5. Changes
We will update this policy if data practices change and revise the "Last updated" date.
